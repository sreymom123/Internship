About the Internship Management System

General Information

The Internship Management System is a platform designed to connect students with internship opportunities from partner companies. This system facilitates the entire process from application, selection, to progress tracking.

Student Role

  • Apply for internships to partner companies
  • Track application status
  • Download internship-related documents
  • Submit weekly progress reports
  • Communicate with supervisors

Manager Role

  • Manage student and company accounts
  • Post new internship opportunities
  • Monitor all internship processes
  • Provide reports and statistics
  • Resolve various issues
